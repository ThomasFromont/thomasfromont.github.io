1. How this policy is organised

Sections 3 to 10 describe practices that apply to every app listed above. Section 2 describes each app individually, because the data an app handles depends on what that app does. Where the two differ, the app-specific section prevails.

Across all our apps, three commitments hold without exception: we never sell your personal information, we never share it with third parties for their own marketing, and we collect only what the app needs to work or to be improved.

2. The apps covered by this policy

My Caps (Capsy in Italian)

com.icecode.mycaps (Google Play) · com.tfromont.MyCaps (App Store) · rated 18+

My Caps is a beer cap collection app. You photograph your caps, organise them, browse breweries on a map, and track statistics by brewery and country. My Caps has no user account and no sign-up.

Stored on your device

  • Photos of your caps, taken with the camera or imported from your photo library. Image processing — isolating a cap from its background — runs entirely on your device.
  • Your collection: the caps you have added, their brewery and country, and any details you record.
  • Your app preferences and settings.

Synced through your own iCloud account (iPhone)

  • Your collection, including the cut-out cap images. On iPhone, My Caps syncs your collection automatically when you are signed in to iCloud; there is no separate setting in the app. It is stored in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account, which Apple provides to you under its own terms. We have no access to it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for My Caps in your iPhone's iCloud settings. The Android app does not use iCloud.

Leaves your device

  • Usage analytics and crash reports, as described in sections 3 and 4.
  • Brewery data. Browsing breweries downloads the brewery catalogue from our hosting provider (Google Firebase). Reporting a missing brewery sends the name and country you enter as an analytics event (section 3). Neither includes your photos.

Not collected by this app

  • Your location. My Caps does not request location permission and does not track where you are. The map shows brewery locations, not yours.
  • Your photos. Cap photos are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Your name, email address or phone number, unless you choose to email us for support.

Permissions requested

  • Camera — to photograph your caps. Requested only when you choose to add a cap with the camera, and you can decline.
  • Photo library access — used only for the specific images you select when importing a cap.
  • Internet — to load brewery data and map tiles, and to send analytics and crash reports.

3. Analytics

Our apps use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how they are used in aggregate — which screens are opened, which features are used, and general device information such as device model, operating system version, language and approximate region derived from your IP address. In My Caps, some events also include the brewery and country of a cap you add or move, the text of a brewery search that found nothing, and the name and country of a brewery you report. Our apps do not access your device's advertising identifier.

Our apps do not display advertising, and analytics data is not used for advertising or ad targeting.

4. Crash and performance reports

Our apps use Firebase Crashlytics to receive automatic diagnostic reports when an app crashes or misbehaves. These reports contain technical information such as the crash stack trace, device model and operating system version, and are used solely to find and fix defects.

5. What we never collect

Regardless of the app, we do not collect your contacts, messages, calendar, health data, or financial information. Any data an individual app does handle is listed in its entry in section 2.

6. Service providers

We rely on the following third parties, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google — Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google Maps and ML Kit (privacy policy).

On iPhone, My Caps syncs through iCloud, a service Apple provides to you directly under your Apple Account and iCloud terms (privacy policy). Apple does not process this data on our behalf, and we have no access to it.

7. Data retention

Data stored on your device is kept for as long as the app is installed; uninstalling removes it from that device. On iPhone with iCloud sync on, your collection also stays in your iCloud until you delete the caps in the app or delete My Caps' data from your iCloud storage settings. Device backups (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Android's automatic backup to your Google Account) are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not by this one.

Analytics and crash data are retained by Google for a limited period in accordance with Firebase's data retention settings, after which they are deleted automatically.

8. Your rights

You can delete any cap from within the app. On iPhone with iCloud sync on, the deletion also applies to your iCloud copy and your other devices. Uninstalling the app removes its data from that device, but not from your iCloud; to remove the iCloud copy, delete My Caps' data from your iCloud storage settings. We cannot access or delete your iCloud data for you. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export or erase personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict its processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@icecode.fr. We respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in France, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

9. Children's privacy

Our apps are intended for adults and are not directed at children. Individual apps may carry a higher age rating where their subject matter requires it — My Caps is rated 18+ because it relates to beer and brewing. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Analytics and crash data may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on the safeguards offered by the relevant service provider. Your iCloud data is stored by Apple, possibly outside your country, as part of the iCloud service it provides to you.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including to cover a newly published app. Any change will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Significant changes will also be announced in the affected app's release notes.

12. Contact us
