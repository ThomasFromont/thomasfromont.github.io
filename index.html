Our apps

Two apps, each built around one thing done well.

My Caps

Beer cap collection · Capsy in Italian

The easiest way to organise and show off a beer cap collection. Snap a cap, link it to its brewery, and My Caps places it on the map and keeps your collection tidy, however large it grows.

  • Add a cap in seconds with the camera or from your photos. The cap is cut out from its background on your phone.
  • Explore your collection on a map and see where every brewery is.
  • Statistics by caps, breweries and countries.
  • No account. Your collection and your photos stay on your device.

Ranker

Leagues and tournaments · Coming soon

Season-long leagues and one-night tournaments. Ranker keeps score for any game you play together: office ping-pong, a tennis ladder, darts at the bar, pétanque. Nobody needs an account. Just share a link.

  • Elo-style leagues that update after every game, with each player's rank over time.
  • Brackets and group stages for a tournament in one evening.
  • No account for anyone, not even the organiser. Share an invite link or a 7-character code.
  • Works offline. Results entered without a connection sync later.

Coming to the App Store and Google Play.

The studio

Small on purpose.

IceCode is the company of Thomas Fromont, a mobile developer based in Paris. Founded in 2022, it designs, builds and publishes its own apps.

Everything is done in-house, from the first sketch to the store listing, the translations and the support emails. One person knows every line of code, so bugs get fixed quickly and your questions reach the person who can answer them.

Native on each platform

SwiftUI on iPhone, Jetpack Compose on Android. Each app follows the conventions of your phone, because it is built for it.

Your data stays yours

No ads, and we never sell data. Each app collects only what it needs and explains it in plain words in its privacy notice.

Made to last

We keep our apps up to date, fix what breaks and translate them carefully. Both apps speak 11 languages.
