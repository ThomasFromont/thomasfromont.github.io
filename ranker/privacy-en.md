# Privacy notice

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

Ranker is built by a solo developer, for people who play together. This notice explains what the app collects, what it does not, and what happens to your data when you leave or delete a Ranker.

## What we collect

Ranker collects only three things:

- **A display name** — the one you choose when you join a Ranker.
- **An anonymous device identifier** — a random ID generated on your device so the app can tell your device apart from others in the same Ranker.
- **Crash reports** — if the app crashes, a technical report (device model, OS version, app version, where in the code the crash happened, and a random installation identifier) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service, so the bug can be fixed. Each time the app opens, Crashlytics also records a short session signal with the same technical details, so crash rates can be measured. Neither ever contains your name, your games or your scores.

That is the whole list. Ranker does **not** collect your email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or biometric data. There are no advertising identifiers, no usage analytics, and no tracking — not across apps, not across the web, not at all.

## How Ranker is organised

Ranker is **peer-managed**. A Ranker belongs to the group that plays in it, not to a company. The person who creates a Ranker can rename it, add players, and delete it; anyone who joins can record results and leave. There is no account, no login, and no central profile that follows you between groups.

## Leaving and deleting

- **Any member can leave a Ranker.** When you leave, your past results stay in that Ranker's history — the games you played and the points you earned remain part of the record, so the standings of everyone you played against stay accurate.
- **The creator can delete a Ranker.** Deleting removes the Ranker and all of its games, for everyone. It cannot be undone.

## Backup retention

When you delete a Ranker, all data is removed from our live systems immediately. Encrypted backups may retain copies for up to 30 days before being permanently overwritten.

## App Store data labels

Ranker's App Store and Google Play data-collection labels mirror exactly what is written here: a display name and an anonymous device identifier, used only to run the app, and crash reports, used only to fix bugs. None of it is linked to your identity, and none of it is used to track you.
