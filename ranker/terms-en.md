# Terms of use

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

Ranker is published by Thomas Fromont (IceCode). Questions: [support@icecode.fr](mailto:support@icecode.fr).

By creating or joining a Ranker, you accept these terms.

## A Ranker is run by its players

There are no accounts: each Ranker is run among peers by the people who share it, and its creator can rename or delete it. You are responsible for what you type in it — the Ranker's name, player names, scores and notes.

## What is not allowed

- Illegal content.
- Hateful or discriminatory content.
- Harassment, insults or threats.
- Sexual content.
- Other people's personal data, beyond a first name or nickname they would agree to.
- Impersonating someone else.
- Spam.

## Reporting a Ranker

In a Ranker, open the Settings tab and tap "Report this Ranker", or email [support@icecode.fr](mailto:support@icecode.fr) with the Ranker's name.

## What we do

We review every report. We may delete a Ranker that breaks these terms, without notice; the deletion is permanent, as when its creator deletes it.

## No warranty

Ranker is a free side project, provided as is, without warranty. The service may change, be interrupted or stop at any time.
