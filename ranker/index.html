Ranker

Rankings and tournaments for the people you play with.

Ranker keeps a ranking or runs a tournament among friends, a club or colleagues: beer pong, pétanque, darts, foosball, a tennis ladder at the office… It is a native app for iOS and Android.

  • Leagues use an ELO-style ranking.
  • Tournaments are brackets.
  • No account, no login. You create a Ranker and share an invite code or link; the group manages it among peers.
  • Scores are free text. The ranking only depends on who beat whom.

Ranker is not yet available on the App Store or Google Play.
