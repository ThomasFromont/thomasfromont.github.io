Leagues & tournaments

Ranker

Season-long leagues. One-night tournaments. Ranker keeps score for any game you play together, and nobody needs an account. Just share a link.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Free · iPhone and Android, foldables included · 11 languages · Light and dark mode

How it works

Create a Ranker, share the link, play.

Leagues that last for months

The ranking updates after every game. Choose an Elo-style rating, where beating a higher-rated player earns you more, or simple points. New players can be added along the way.

Tournaments for one evening

Single-elimination bracket, group stage, or groups followed by a bracket. Forfeits are handled, and you can share the result at the end.

Players, pairs or teams

Play 1v1, or 2v2 in a league with pairs that can change from game to game. Rank several players in one game, or enter teams for a 5-a-side cup.

No accounts for anyone

Not the players, not even the organiser. People join with an invite link or a 7-character code and claim their name.

The score, or just who won

Scores are optional free text, like “11-9, close one”. Wrong result? Undo it. Every game keeps its edit history, and results entered offline sync when you're back online.

A colour for each Ranker

Pick it when you create the Ranker, and every member sees the same one. No streaks, no nagging notifications: a league can sit quiet for weeks.

Privacy

Private by design.

  • No account, no email, no phone number.
  • No tracking, no analytics. Only crash reports, so bugs get fixed.
  • Only your group sees your Ranker. Its content is visible to people who have its invite code.
  • Hosted in Paris, with a way to have your data erased on request.
