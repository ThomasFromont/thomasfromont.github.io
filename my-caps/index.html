My Caps

The app for cap collectors. Photograph your caps, find the brewery behind each one, and watch your collection grow on a map.

Free · iPhone (iOS 26 or later) and Android · 11 languages · Published as Capsy in Italian

What it does

Every cap in its place.

Whether you have twenty caps in a jar or a thousand in display cases, My Caps keeps track of every piece.

Add a cap in seconds

Snap a photo with the camera or import one from your library. The cap is cut out from its background automatically, on your phone.

Find the brewery

Link each cap to its brewery and country. Browse a brewery to see every cap you own from it.

See it on a map

Explore your collection on a world map and discover where your caps come from.

Statistics

Count your caps, breweries and countries at a glance, country by country.

Search everything

Find a cap or a brewery instantly, even in a large collection.

In your language

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Japanese and Korean.

Privacy

Your collection stays on your phone.

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Your photos are never uploaded. Cutting out the cap happens on your device.
  • No location tracking. The map shows breweries, not you.
  • No ads. Usage analytics and crash reports help us improve the app, as the policy explains.
