Design system

Glace & granit

Ice white, granite text, a deep glacier blue for action and copper for rare details. Light first; the dark theme is a fjord at night. Everything on this page is rendered with the real stylesheet, /style.css, and follows your system's light or dark mode.

Logo

{% include logo.svg %}IceCode

A six-armed ice crystal whose branches are code chevrons. The mark is a single stroke drawn in currentColor; the wordmark is Schibsted Grotesk 800 with tight tracking.
