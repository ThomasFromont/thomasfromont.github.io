Foosball stats

Gamelle

Film a foosball set and Gamelle does the rest: it tracks the ball frame by frame, finds the goals and gives you the stats of every rod. No score sheet, nothing to type during the game.

What it does

See where you win the ball, and where you lose it.

Made for players who want to improve: know what your shots, passes and clearances really turn into.

The ball, frame by frame

Import the video of a set from your photos and tap the ball once. Gamelle follows it through the whole set, on your iPhone.

Goals checked in one tap

Gamelle suggests the goals it detected, gamelles included. Watch each goal's action, confirm or reject, add one it missed.

Stats rod by rod

Attack conversion, successful midfield passes, where your clearances go, and how many balls each team played on every rod.

Your tempo

Your usual time before shooting or passing. If it is too regular, the defender can read it.

Your whole season

Group your sets into matches and tournaments, 1 vs 1, 2 vs 2 or team relays. Your player profile adds up your stats, match after match.

Share the result

Send a set's result to your team from the share sheet, to the app and the people you choose.

Easy to film

A phone above the table is all it takes.

  • Film the set with a phone mounted above the table or behind a goal. The filming guide in the app gives you the few rules for a well-analysed video.
  • Import the video into Gamelle and tap the ball once. The analysis takes a few minutes; keep the app open while it runs.
  • Check the goals, and the stats of the set are ready.

Privacy

Your videos stay on your phone.

  • All the analysis runs on your iPhone. No server is involved.
  • No account, no email, no phone number.
  • No data collected. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • A set's video is deleted when you confirm its goals. Its stats are kept.
