# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Gamelle analyzes videos of your foosball matches to produce statistics. It is designed to respect your privacy: **all analysis runs on your iPhone, and no data is collected.**

## In short

- **100% on-device analysis.** No server is involved. The app sends nothing over the internet.
- **No account.** No sign-up, no login: the app works without an account, email address or phone number.
- **No tracking, no data collection.** No ads, no trackers, no usage analytics. No data is collected or shared, with us or with any third party.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **The videos you import.** You pick a video from your photo library with the iOS picker: Gamelle receives only that video and has no access to the rest of your library. It keeps a copy inside the app, excluded from iCloud backups, and deletes it when you confirm the set's goals.
- **Your matches and statistics**: sets, matches, tournaments, the player names you enter, goals, statistics and calibration settings.
- **Your preferences** in the app.

This data is stored only on your device. We have no access to it. It may be included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), which is then governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## What you choose to send

- **Sharing a result.** When you share a set's result, you pick the app and the recipient in the iOS share sheet.
- **Contacting us.** If you write to us (from the app or at support@icecode.fr), we receive your email address and message, used only to reply.
- **Apple crash reports.** If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may pass anonymized crash reports on to us. That choice and those reports are Apple's, not Gamelle's.

## Permissions

Gamelle requests no permissions: no camera, no full photo library access, no location, no tracking.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on your iPhone as long as the app is installed. You can delete a set, a match or a tournament in the app; uninstalling Gamelle removes all its data from the device.

## Children

Gamelle collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

We hold no data about you, apart from emails you send us. For any question, or to exercise your rights (access, correction, erasure) over those emails, write to [support@icecode.fr](mailto:support@icecode.fr). We reply within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in France, the CNIL ([www.cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its update date.
